# Account Health and Service Review Playbook

## Health score
Every account has a health score from 0 to 100, recalculated daily from the unified reporting dataset. Points are deducted for SLA breaches in the last 90 days, overdue debt, network outage minutes in the last three months and low customer satisfaction (CSAT). Points are added for growing data usage. Scores below 40 are high risk, 40 to 69 are watch, and 70 or above are healthy.

## High-risk accounts
When an account drops below 40, the account manager must book a service review call within 5 working days. Prepare by reviewing open tickets, outage history, overdue invoices and CSAT comments. The aim of the call is to listen, agree a recovery plan with dated actions and confirm a follow-up date.

## Service review agenda
1. Customer's priorities and any changes in their business. 2. Service performance against SLA, including an honest account of breaches. 3. Billing questions. 4. Agreed actions and owners. 5. Opportunities to improve their setup, raised only once service issues are resolved.

## Growth opportunities
Healthy accounts (70 or above) with rising data usage and a single product line are candidates for an upgrade conversation, for example a leased line upgrade, cloud hosting or a managed IT support bundle. Never propose an upsell to an account with open SLA breaches.
