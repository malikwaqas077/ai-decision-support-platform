# Product Catalogue

Cloud-hosted business telephony with call recording, hunt groups and Microsoft Teams integration. Billed per seat per month. Typical SME bundle £180 to £900 per month.

## Leased Line
Dedicated, uncontended fibre connectivity from 100 Mbps to 10 Gbps with a 4-hour fix SLA. Typical monthly charge £350 to £1,400.

## Cloud Hosting
Virtual servers and managed backup hosted in our Teesside data centre, with UK data residency. Typical monthly charge £250 to £2,200.

## Managed IT Support
Service desk, proactive monitoring, patching and on-site engineer visits under the SLA policy. Typical monthly charge £400 to £3,000.

## Microsoft 365 Licences
Licensing and administration for Microsoft 365 business plans. Typical monthly charge £90 to £800.

## Data Centre Colocation
Rack space, power and cooling in our Tier III facility with 24/7 access and remote hands. Typical monthly charge £600 to £2,600.
