# Service Level Agreement Policy

## Response and resolution targets
All managed-service customers are covered by priority-based resolution targets. P1 (service down, whole site affected): 4 hours. P2 (major degradation or single critical user): 8 hours. P3 (standard fault or request): 24 hours. P4 (minor issue, information request): 72 hours. Targets run 24/7 for P1 and business hours for P2 to P4.

## Measuring SLA compliance
A ticket breaches SLA when its resolution time exceeds the target for its priority. Tickets still open beyond the target also count as breached. The company target is 95% of tickets resolved within SLA over any rolling 90-day period.

## Service credits
A customer with three or more SLA breaches in a rolling 90-day period is entitled to a service credit of 5% of that month's managed-service charge, rising to 10% for five or more breaches. Credits are approved by the Service Desk Lead and applied to the next invoice. Credits must be offered proactively; customers should not have to ask.

## Escalation path
P1 tickets are escalated to the on-call engineer immediately and to the Service Desk Lead after 2 hours. Repeated breaches for one customer trigger a root-cause review led by the Service Desk Lead, with the account manager informed within one working day.
