# Credit Control Procedure

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are 30 days from invoice date. Direct debit customers are collected on the 1st working day after the invoice date.

## Overdue stages
At 31 days, an automated reminder email is sent. At 45 days, finance phones the customer's accounts contact and informs the account manager. At 60 days, a formal letter is sent and the account manager must agree a payment plan with the customer. At 90 days, the account is referred to the Finance Director.

## Credit hold
Accounts with more than £5,000 overdue beyond 45 days are placed on credit hold: no new orders or upgrades are processed until the balance is cleared or a payment plan is agreed. Existing services are never suspended without Finance Director approval, because connectivity and VoIP are business-critical for our customers.

## Linking debt to service issues
Before chasing a customer with open SLA breaches, finance must check with the account manager. Late payment is often a sign of service dissatisfaction, and a service credit may resolve both issues.
